Vision

It listens the way a teacher does — not just what note, but how.

String Sense clips to the bridge without altering the instrument's voice, and reads the physical signature of a performance directly off the wood: bow onset, intonation drift, tone shape, the shape of a phrase.

Live, it stays quiet — a glance, not a distraction. After the session, it writes what a good teacher would say in the margins: the two or three things worth fixing next, pitched to where the player actually is.

Product

Two halves of one instrument

A clip-on sensor built to a luthier's tolerances, paired with a companion app that turns raw signal into practice you can use.

Hardware

The Pickup

A silicone-and-cork clamp that adapts across the bridge scale from violin to cello, with dual piezo elements and on-board DSP feeding a low-latency BLE link.

Input impedance≥ 10 MΩ
Capture24-bit / 44.1 kHz PCM
System latency≤ 50 ms end-to-end
Battery life~6.5 hrs active
Weight≤ 20 g

Feedback

Two speeds of listening

While you play

Lightweight signals at the edge of your vision flag drifting intonation, a rushed entrance, or a heavy bow — quickly enough to act on, quiet enough not to break concentration.

After you stop

A full written report, structured like real teacher's notes: the two or three moments that mattered most, timestamped, in language pitched to exactly where you are.

Same passage, from the session report —

Nice work today! Your G string passage was a little under pitch in a few spots — slow down there and really listen before you move your finger. You're getting closer every time.
